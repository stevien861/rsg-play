Clean Sweep: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Clean Sweep is a street-cleaning game for Android published by RSG Play ("we"). This page explains what information the game uses and why. In short: there are no passwords or accounts of our own, adverts only play if you choose to watch one, there are no analytics or tracking tools, and your progress stays on your phone unless you sign in to Google Play Games.

Information the game uses

Reminders (only if you say yes). If you turn reminders on, the game sets them up on your phone itself (for example "your free gift is ready"). Nothing is sent to us or anyone else, and you can turn them off in the game's Settings or your phone's Settings.

Game progress. Your cash, gems, upgrades, stars and settings are saved on your phone. We cannot see them. If your phone has Android's backup switched on, Google may include this data in your device backup under your own Google account.

Google Play Games (optional). If you sign in to Google Play Games, the game stores a copy of your game progress in your Google account (Google's "Saved Games") so it comes back after a reinstall or on a new phone. Sign-in is handled by Google; the game does not see your password or email address, and we do not run any servers that hold your progress. Google's privacy policy applies to Google Play Games.

Before & after photos. Pictures of the streets you clean are saved in the game's private storage on your phone. If you tap Share, the picture is added to your phone's photo gallery so the app you choose (for example WhatsApp) can send it. Nothing is shared unless you tap Share.

Map data. The streets in the game come from OpenStreetMap and Overture Maps. Most are packed inside the app. A few areas are fetched from public OpenStreetMap map servers the first time you open them: the request says which piece of the map is needed (the place in the game, never where you are), and, like any website, that server can see your phone's internet address. The game does not need your location and never asks for it.

Adverts (only if you choose)

Some bonuses can be doubled by watching an advert, for example doubling a job's pay. Adverts only ever play when you tap a "watch an ad" button, and you can always carry on without. These adverts come from Google AdMob, which may use your phone's advertising ID and collect information about your device to show and measure adverts. Players in the UK, the EEA and Switzerland are asked for consent first, using Google's consent message, and can change their choice at any time from "Advert choices" in the Store. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

If you buy something in the game, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card details. Google gives us only what is needed to deliver the purchase.

What we do not do

  • No accounts or passwords of our own (Google Play Games sign-in is optional).
  • No adverts that play by themselves.
  • No analytics or tracking tools.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information.

Children

Clean Sweep is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

Uninstalling the game removes everything it has saved on your phone, including any before & after pictures (pictures you shared stay in your gallery). Progress saved to Google Play Games stays in your Google account; you can delete it from the Google Play Games app or your Google account settings.

Changes

If the game starts using information in a new way, we will update this page and change the date at the top.
